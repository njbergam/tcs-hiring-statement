**UCLA Computer Science**: [Multiple Tenure-track Assistant Professor Positions in Computer Science](https://recruit.apo.ucla.edu/JPF11410) (posted November 9, 2026; [saved PDF](hiring-calls-alignment/ucla-cs-hiring.pdf))

> In evaluating candidates, the department values a strong record of meaningful research accomplishments over the number of papers published at top conference venues. Applicants are encouraged to identify in their research statement up to three contributions they consider most significant and to explain their impact.
>
> In addition to a research talk and one-on-one meetings, interviews include a one-hour "deep dive" session in which a small group of faculty experts asks specific questions about the candidate's work, giving the candidate an opportunity to show the depth of their understanding.

**Johns Hopkins Mathematics**: [Hiring for research-intensive positions in the age of AI](https://mathematics.jhu.edu/about/jobs/#AI-hiring) (announced November 20, 2026 in [Math in the age of AI](https://mathematics.jhu.edu/2026/11/20/math-in-the-age-of-ai/); applies to research-intensive positions at all levels; [saved PDF](hiring-calls-alignment/jhu-math-hiring.pdf))

> We do not assign positive weight to written work in the absence of evidence that the authors understand its contents, regardless of the significance of the results.
>
> In general, we evaluate candidates on their most significant contributions, including the quality of their mathematical writing, without necessarily taking into account all of their work.
>
> The department expects transparency from the candidates regarding AI use in mathematical research, but does not evaluate them on the basis of an a priori preference for or against the use of AI, or regarding the extent of it.
