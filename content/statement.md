In the evaluation of future tenure-track hires in my field, I support the following:[^1]

1. **Emphasize quality over quantity.** In the evaluations of candidates, consider at most three works from July 2026 onwards (of the candidates' choosing), in addition to any number of prior works before July 2026.
2. **Actively assess thorough understanding.** Evaluate the thoroughness of the candidates' understanding of the aforementioned works more than was done normally until now. This evaluation could take the form of a "chalk talk," more mathematically substantial interview slots, and/or other methods of the department's choosing.

[^1]: In a manner consistent with university guidelines.
